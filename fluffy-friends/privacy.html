Fluffy Friends: My 3D Pet – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short: Fluffy Friends: My 3D Pet is a game for children. We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information. Game progress is saved only on the device. The Android app shows family-safe, non-personalised ads from Google AdMob.

Who we are

Fluffy Friends: My 3D Pet is published by Equity Holdings. Email: mentalbloom.help@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game has no accounts, no chat, no camera or microphone access, no location access and no way to contact other players. Pet names typed by players stay on the device and are never sent to us.

Game progress (pets, coins and items) is stored only in the app's local storage on the device. Deleting the app, or using "Reset all progress" in the grown-ups section, removes it.

Talking with your pet

Children talk to their pet by tapping ready-made phrases. There is no free typing and the game never uses the microphone or camera. If read-aloud is switched on, the pet's replies are spoken by the device's own built-in text-to-speech. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Advertising

The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob so that the game can stay free. Because the game is designed for children:

  • every ad request is marked as child-directed and under the age of consent;
  • ads are non-personalised and limited to content suitable for general audiences (rated G);
  • the app does not use the device's advertising ID, and does not use interest-based advertising or remarketing;
  • ads are shown only on menu screens and at natural breaks after a mini-game, never during play.

To deliver ads and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information, such as the device's IP address and app information. Google's handling of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In-app purchases

There are no purchases with real money. All coins and items are earned by playing.

Links

The only links that leave the game are inside the grown-ups section, which is protected by a question for adults.

Children's privacy

We follow the Google Play Families policy and aim to comply with children's privacy laws, including the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact
